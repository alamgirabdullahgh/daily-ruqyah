# Quran root-word data (Research page)

`quran-roots.json` and `quran-ayat.json` are generated by `tools/build-quran-roots.py` from
[mustafa0x/quran-morphology](https://github.com/mustafa0x/quran-morphology), a fork of the
Quranic Arabic Corpus morphology v0.4 (Kais Dukes, https://corpus.quran.com) - licensed under the GNU General Public License.
Root assignments are research data, not a fatwa/tafsir source; verify against classical lexicons where it matters.

## Waqf marks (Research page, audio cut)

- `quran-waqf.json`: positions of the waqf (stop) signs per verse, built by `tools/build-quran-waqf.py` from the Tanzil.net Uthmani Hafs text (CC BY 3.0, unmodified wording, see tanzil.net/docs/text_license) taken through [fawazahmed0/quran-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/quran-api) (`ara-quranuthmanihaf`). 13 verses whose word count does not match are skipped.

## Bangla (Research page)

- `quran-wbw-bn.json` and the word meanings inside `quran-roots.json`: from
  [SadaqaWorks/Word-By-Word-Quran-Android](https://github.com/SadaqaWorks/Word-By-Word-Quran-Android) (MIT), `wordbyword.db`.
- `quran-bn.json`: Bangla verse translation by Muhiuddin Khan, taken from [tanzil.net](https://tanzil.net) through
  [risan/quran-json](https://github.com/risan/quran-json) (CC BY-SA 4.0). Built by `tools/build-quran-bn.py`.

- `quran-chunks/b/`: in 19 verses (surahs 6, 11, 15, 17, 21, 24, 29, 31, 44, 48, 114) sadhu words were made colloquial (e.g. "ইহা" to "এটা"), and in 26 verses broken characters from the source (damaged HTML entities) were repaired by hand; a derivative of a CC BY-SA 4.0 work, therefore shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 with the same attribution.
